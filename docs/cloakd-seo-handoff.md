# Cloakd SEO Handoff

## Mission
Rank Cloakd higher than SenesTech and resellers for Evolve, rodent birth control, rodent fertility control, and related buyer-intent searches.

Primary conversion goal: direct ecommerce purchases of Cloakd's Evolve starter kits and refills.

Positioning: Cloakd is the specialist reseller for Evolve, with clearer deployment guidance, better buyer education, stronger product selection help, and a useful public-data asset in Rodent Radar.

## Non-Negotiables
- Do not rewrite PDP body copy until the PDPs have been audited and proposed changes are reviewed.
- Preserve the current PDPs as the conversion endpoint unless the user approves specific changes.
- Treat the first PDP pass as technical SEO and schema cleanup only.
- Use evidence-led claims. Source product, safety, EPA, and field-result language.
- Do not imply Cloakd manufactures Evolve.
- Do not use Rodent Radar data as a disease-risk or rat-population claim.
- Prefer compact buyer-intent pages over broad informational content.
- Build authority through Rodent Radar, comparison pages, source-backed education, and internal links into PDPs.

## Copy Rules
Apply `/Users/tounshoyoye/Documents/cloakd-removals/seo-source-material/Do Not Write Like This.md` to all SEO work.

Avoid:
- "No X. No Y. Just Z."
- "It's not about X, it's about Y."
- overused three-item rhythm
- "game-changer", "supercharge", and similar startup filler
- repeated "real"
- emojis
- arrow overload
- corporate verbs like "leverage", "utilize", "facilitate", "highlight"
- hedging openers like "It's worth considering"
- fake short hook questions
- "Enter: [thing]"
- "here's the kicker"
- em dash overload
- vague lines like "changed everything"
- literary-analysis language such as "this symbolizes"

Write plainly. Use specifics, numbers, examples, and direct buyer language.

## Edward Sturm / Skill Doctrine To Apply
- Satisfy search intent immediately above the fold.
- Reduce pogo-sticking with a clear H1, relevant image/product visual, short answer, and CTA.
- Build bottom-funnel pages first.
- Use compact keyword pages for buyers who want a solution but do not yet know Cloakd.
- Use linkable assets for authority, then pass authority internally to commercial pages.
- Do not rely on backlinks alone; relevance and user signals matter.
- Avoid blasting links to PDPs. Favor homepage, Rodent Radar, comparison hubs, and category pages, then use internal linking.
- Build pages and comparison tables that LLMs can parse and cite.
- Use GSC for striking-distance keywords once Cloakd has ranking data.
