# Homepage Repositioning Proposal

Date: 2026-05-26  
Status: proposal only. No homepage copy or layout has been changed.

## Goal

Reposition the homepage from a managed rodent fertility-control service funnel into an ecommerce-first homepage for buying Evolve through Cloakd.

The homepage should make one thing clear above the fold:

Cloakd sells Evolve starter kits and refills, with plain deployment guidance that helps buyers choose, place, and keep the bait stocked correctly.

## Why This Change Matters

The current homepage still reflects the older business model:

- H1: `Every six weeks, the rodents are back. We end that cycle.`
- Primary CTA: `Get started`
- Above-fold conversion element: `Site Walkthrough & Program Estimate`
- Major sections: field data, walkthrough, program, service areas, operator reviews
- Nav framing: `Program`, `Solutions`, `Compliance`, `Areas`

That page can still support trust, but it does not immediately satisfy the buyer searching for:

- `evolve rat birth control`
- `evolve mouse birth control`
- `evolve rodent birth control`
- `rat birth control`
- `mouse birth control`
- `where to buy evolve`
- `evolve soft bait`

The site now needs the homepage to behave like a specialist commerce hub, not a service lead-gen page.

## Positioning

Recommended homepage position:

> Buy Evolve rodent birth control from a specialist source that explains how to use it correctly.

Supporting idea:

> Evolve is the product. Cloakd helps you choose the right kit, keep stations stocked, and understand what should happen over the first 30, 60, and 90 days.

Do not imply Cloakd manufactures Evolve. Use language such as:

- `Evolve products sold by Cloakd`
- `Evolve starter kits and refills`
- `Cloakd helps you deploy Evolve correctly`
- `Specialist guidance for Evolve rodent fertility control`

Avoid:

- implying Cloakd is the original product maker
- implying service coverage is the main offer
- making Rodent Radar sound like a disease-risk tool
- claiming estimated rat population sizes

## Proposed Homepage Flow

1. **Hero: product-first**
   - Product image or starter-kit/refill visual.
   - Clear H1.
   - Starter Kit and Refill CTAs.
   - Short trust row: `EPA minimum-risk category`, `rat and mouse options`, `ships in 24 hours` if operationally true.

2. **Find Your Setup**
   - Keep or adapt `FindYourFit` from PDP components if appropriate.
   - Buyer chooses:
     - `I need stations and bait`
     - `I already have stations`
     - `I am treating rats`
     - `I am treating mice`

3. **Why Evolve**
   - Explain replacement cycle briefly.
   - Link to `/why-it-keeps-coming-back`.
   - Keep this section buyer-focused, not service-focused.

4. **How To Use It**
   - Placement guidance:
     - along walls
     - near burrows
     - near dumpsters or trash areas
     - where droppings or travel paths already exist
   - Refill guidance:
     - keep bait available
     - do not let stations run empty
     - expect reproductive effects over breeding cycles

5. **Product Comparison**
   - Starter Kit vs Refill.
   - Rat vs Mouse.
   - 6 lb vs 12 lb.
   - Link directly to PDP variants.

6. **Evidence and Safety**
   - Use existing field-result framing only when sourced.
   - Keep safety claims aligned with PDP safety claims and EPA minimum-risk language.
   - Link to `/evolve-rodent-birth-control` and `/does-rat-birth-control-work`.

7. **Rodent Radar Authority Block**
   - Position as a public-data resource, not the main shopping path.
   - Suggested copy:
     `Rodent Radar tracks official rodent activity reports and civic conditions in verified cities. Use it to understand public reporting patterns near you.`
   - CTA: `Open Rodent Radar`
   - Secondary link: `Estimate a colony trajectory`

8. **Reviews**
   - Keep product/customer reviews if they are tied to Evolve or Cloakd purchase experience.
   - Avoid making service-program reviews the dominant proof.

9. **FAQ**
   - Focus on ecommerce questions:
     - Do I need a license?
     - Which kit should I buy?
     - Does Evolve work for rats and mice?
     - Can I use it with traps?
     - How often should I refill?
     - Is it safe around pets and wildlife when used as directed?

10. **Final CTA**
   - `Start with the kit` and `Buy refills`
   - Optional: `Ask Cloakd for placement help`

Keep the current service/program pages alive for now, but reframe them over time as deployment education, compliance context, and use-case pages that support product purchase.
